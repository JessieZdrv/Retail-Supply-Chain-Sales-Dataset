This project analyzes the Retail Supply Chain Sales Dataset from Kaggle, with a focus on exploring product categories and customer behavior. The initial data exploration aimed to understand the structure of the dataset and gain insights into product types, categories, and sales distribution. After developing a clearer picture of the product landscape, the analysis shifted toward customer segmentation using K-Means clustering, aiming to group customers based on their purchasing patterns. The project showcases how exploratory analysis and unsupervised learning techniques can be used to derive business insights in the retail and supply chain domain.
